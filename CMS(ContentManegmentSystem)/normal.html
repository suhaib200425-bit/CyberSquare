ABOUT THE PROGRAM

Education that creates complete flexibility and real change

The Positive Change Program was created by two Newcastle based solicitors who saw a gap in educational courses available to those charged with domestic violence offences in a local court setting.

Using their experience and knowledge, they created this course to help those charged start their journey of positive change — because real change starts with real understanding.

It is well known that education and rehabilitation are far more effective than incarceration. This program is built to be practical, engaging and easy to understand.
