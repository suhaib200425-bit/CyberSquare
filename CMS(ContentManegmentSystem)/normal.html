ABOUT THE PROGRAM

A program built for genuine reflection.

The Positive Change Program is an eight-module online course developed by criminal lawyers in Newcastle. It was created in response to a clear need — people facing domestic violence charges often want to take meaningful steps forward but don't know where to start.

The course is delivered entirely online through a secure learning platform. You can complete it from anywhere in Australia, at the pace that suits you.
